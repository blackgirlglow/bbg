LOVE & CONNECTION

Healthy Love

Healthy love should feel like a space where both people can grow, communicate honestly and feel respected. It isn't about finding a perfect person, but about building a healthy connection together.

Communication matters.

Strong relationships are built on honest communication. Talk about your feelings, listen to understand and create space for difficult conversations instead of avoiding them. You should be able to express yourself without constantly feeling afraid of being dismissed.

Respect is essential.

Love without respect cannot create a healthy foundation. Respect means valuing each other's opinions, boundaries, time and individuality. You can disagree without tearing each other down.

Grow together, not apart from yourself.

A healthy relationship should not require you to lose who you are. Continue pursuing your goals, maintaining your friendships and developing as an individual while building a life with someone else.

👯🏾‍♀️

Friendships

Good friendships bring laughter, support and a sense of belonging. The people around you can have a meaningful influence on the way you experience life.

Choose mutual friendships.

Healthy friendships should not depend entirely on one person doing all the calling, checking in and making plans. Both people should make an effort to care for and maintain the relationship.

Celebrate each other.

A good friend can be happy for your growth without turning every success into competition. Celebrate the people you love and allow yourself to receive that same support.

Make room for honest conversations.

Friendship doesn't mean agreeing about everything. Sometimes caring for a friendship means having an honest conversation, apologizing when necessary and being willing to listen and grow.

💌

Dating & Connection

Dating is an opportunity to learn about another person while also learning more about yourself, your values and the kind of relationship you want to build.

Know what matters to you.

Before getting lost in the excitement of a new connection, take time to understand your own values, boundaries and expectations. Knowing yourself can help you make more intentional decisions.

Pay attention to consistency.

Words can be meaningful, but consistent actions often tell you more about how someone shows up in a relationship. Pay attention to whether their actions match what they say.

Don't abandon yourself for connection.

A new relationship should not require you to ignore your boundaries or shrink yourself to be accepted. The right connection should leave room for honesty, individuality and mutual respect.
